HealthCity Trauma Centre & Superspeciality Hospital

India, Lucknow

Multi Speciality

HealthCity Trauma Centre & Superspeciality Hospital is a NABH-accredited, 300+ bedded hospital in Lucknow, offering advanced trauma care and a wide range of superspeciality services. The hospital is equipped with state-of-the-art operation theatres, modular ICUs, and a dedicated team of highly qualified doctors and paramedical staff. Key specialties include Trauma & Emergency, Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopaedics, Gastroenterology, Oncology, Nephrology, Urology, and more. The hospital is committed to providing quality healthcare with compassion and excellence.

  • Address:Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India
  • No Of Beds: 300+
  • Facilities: NABH Accredited, 24x7 Trauma & Emergency, Modular Operation Theatres, Advanced ICU, Cardiac Care Unit, Dialysis, Cath Lab, Radiology & Imaging, Blood Bank, Pharmacy, Ambulance Services, International Patient Desk
