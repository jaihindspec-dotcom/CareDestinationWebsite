HealthCity Trauma Centre & Superspeciality Hospital

India, Lucknow

Multi Speciality

HealthCity Trauma Centre & Superspeciality Hospital is a NABH-accredited, 300+ bedded hospital in Lucknow, offering advanced trauma care and a wide range of superspeciality services. The hospital is equipped with state-of-the-art operation theatres, modular ICUs, and a dedicated team of highly qualified doctors and paramedical staff.
