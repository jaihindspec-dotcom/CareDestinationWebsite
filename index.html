Specializations

At CareDestinations, we bring together expert medical care and restorative travel experiences. Our medical tourism services cover a wide range of treatments for all body systems, focusing on holistic healing and overall wellness. We’re dedicated to guiding our clients on their journey to recovery and a healthier, more fulfilling life.

Orthopaedics

Orthopaedics is the branch of medicine that focuses on the musculoskeletal system — including bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, tendons, and nerves. We offer joint replacement, trauma care and minimally invasive orthopaedic procedures.

Gynaecology

Gynaecology focuses on the health of the female reproductive system — including the uterus, ovaries, fallopian tubes, cervix, and vagina. Our services include fertility care, minimally invasive surgery and high-risk pregnancy management.

Visa & Travel Documentation

  • Invitation & medical support letters
  • Embassy / consulate guidance
  • Appointment scheduling & document check

Language & Interpretation

Professional interpreters and language support during consultations, admission, procedures and discharge.

  • On-site and remote interpreters
  • Medical document translation

Currency & Payment Support

SIM & Connectivity

Accommodation Options

Curated accommodation near hospitals ranging from patient-friendly hotels to serviced apartments and guesthouses.

  • Hospital-affiliated hotels & on-campus rooms
  • Long-stay serviced apartments for companions

Pre-Travel Consultations

Virtual and in-person pre-travel medical consultations to finalise treatment plans and prepare for the journey.

  • Teleconsultations with partner doctors
  • Medical record review & pre-op instructions

Telemedicine & Remote Follow-up

Post-treatment telemedicine consultations to monitor recovery, adjust medications and provide rehabilitation guidance.

  • Scheduled follow-ups via video or phone
  • Remote wound checks, medication reviews
  • Coordination with local physicians at home

Blood & Special Supply Arrangements

Assistance securing blood units, special implants, or rare donor matches as required by the treating team.

  • Hospital blood bank coordination
  • Directed donor arrangements where needed
  • Assistance with imports / special supplies

Private Duty Nursing & Care

  • Short-term and extended nursing care
  • Physiotherapy & rehabilitation referrals
  • Care plans tailored to clinical orders

Accessibility & Mobility Support

Wheelchairs, mobility aids and special transfer arrangements are available to make hospital visits and transfers safer.

  • Wheelchair and stretcher arrangements
